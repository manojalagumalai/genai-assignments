📋 How to Use This Tool ▼

    Explore the Banking Domain examples below to understand the expected format for user stories.
  • Copy Sample Stories (Optional)
    Click the "Copy Sample Stories" button to copy pre-written banking examples to your clipboard, or paste your own user stories.
  • Open Langflow Chat Widget
    Look for the Langflow Chat Icon in the top right corner of the screen. Click it to open the chat interface.
  • Paste Your User Stories
    Paste your user stories or requirements into the Langflow chat window. Be descriptive for better results!
  • Generate Test Cases
    The AI will process your input and generate comprehensive test cases. Wait for the AI to complete the analysis.
  • Copy Generated Output
    Once test cases appear on the right side, click the "Copy Output" button to copy them to your clipboard for use in your project.
  • Clear & Restart
    Use the "Clear Chat" button anytime to reset and start with a new batch of test cases.
